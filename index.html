Chicken Stir-Fry

Quick and healthy stir-fry with colorful vegetables

  • 1 500g chicken breast, sliced
  • 2 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 3 1 broccoli head, florets
  • 4 2 carrots,julienned
  • 5 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 6 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 7 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 8 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 9 Fresh ginger, grated

  1. Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil for the sauce.

  2. Heat wok over high heat with oil. Cook chicken until golden, remove and set aside.

  3. Add more oil if needed. Stir-fry garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.

  4. Add vegetables, starting with hardest ones (carrots, broccoli). Cook for 3-4 minutes.

  5. Return chicken to wok, add bell peppers and sauce. Toss for 2 minutes.

  6. Serve immediately over steamed rice or noodles.

Margherita Pizza

Classic Italian pizza with fresh mozzarella and basil

Extended Preparation Time

This recipe requires more than 45 minutes to prepare. Plan accordingly!

  • 1 300g pizza dough
  • 2 200g crushed tomatoes
  • 3 250g fresh mozzarella
  • 4 Fresh basil leaves
  • 5 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 6 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 7 Salt and pepper to taste
  • 8 Parmesan cheese for topping

  1. Let pizza dough come to room temperature and rest for 1 hour.

  2. Preheat oven to maximum temperature (usually 250°C/480°F).

  3. Mix crushed tomatoes with olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper for the sauce.

  4. Roll out dough on a floured surface to desired thickness.

  5. Spread tomato sauce, add torn mozzarella pieces, and drizzle with olive oil.

  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes until crust is golden. Top with fresh basil and parmesan.

Thai Green Curry

Vibrant and aromatic curry with vegetables and coconut milk

  • 1 2 tablespoons green curry paste
  • 2 400ml coconut milk
  • 3 300g chicken breast, sliced
  • 4 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 5 100g tablespoon olive oil
  • 6 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 7 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 8 1 tablespoon palm sugar
  • 9 Fresh Thai basil leaves

  1. Heat a large pot or wok over medium heat. Add curry paste and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

  2. Add sliced chicken and cook until no longer pink, about 5 minutes.

  3. Add remaining coconut milk, vegetables, fish sauce, and palm sugar.

  4. Simmer for 15-20 minutes until vegetables are tender and sauce has thickened.

  5. Stir in fresh Thai basil leaves. Serve hot with jasmine rice.

  6. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve with steamed vegetables or rice.

Creamy Spaghetti Carbonara

A classic Italian pasta dish with eggs, cheese, and pancetta

  • 1 400g spaghetti pasta
  • 2 200g pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 3 4 large eggs
  • 5 100g tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 50g Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 6 Freshly ground black pepper
  • 7 Salt for pasta water

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente.

  2. While pasta cooks, heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add diced pancetta and cook until crispy, about 5-7 minutes.

  3. In a bowl, whisk together eggs, grated Pecorino Romano, and Parmesan cheese. Add plenty of freshly ground black pepper.

  4. Reserve 1 cup of pasta cooking water before draining. Drain pasta and immediately add to the skillet with pancetta.

  5. Remove skillet from heat. Quickly pour in egg mixture while tossing pasta vigorously. Add reserved pasta water as needed to create a creamy sauce.

  6. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve with steamed vegetables or rice.

  • Work quickly when mixing eggs with hot pasta to avoid scrambling

  • Reserve extra pasta water - it's the secret to perfect creaminess

  • Use full-fat coconut milk for richest, creamiest sauce

Caprese Sandwich

Fresh Italian sandwich with mozzarella, tomato, and basil

  • 1 1 ciabatta bread
  • 2 200g pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 3 2 large eggs
  • 5 Fresh basil leaves
  • 4 3 tablespoons pesto
  • 6 Freshly ground black pepper
  • 7 Olive oil
  • 8 Salt for pasta water

  1. Slice ciabatta bread in half horizontally.

  2. Toast bread lightly until just crispy.

  3. Spread pesto on both sides of bread.

  4. Layer mozzarella slices, tomato slices, and fresh basil leaves.

  5. Drizzle with olive oil and balsamic glaze. Season with salt and pepper.

  6. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve with steamed vegetables or rice.

Greek Moussaka

Traditional layered eggplant casserole with lamb

Extended Preparation Time

This recipe requires more than 45 minutes to prepare. Plan accordingly!

  • 1 3 large eggplants, sliced
  • 2 500g ground lamb
  • 3 400 large eggs
  • 5 Fresh basil leaves
  • 4 3 tablespoons pesto
  • 6 500ml béchamel sauce
  • 7 100g parmesan cheese
  • 8 Salt for pasta water
  • 9 Olive oil

  1. Slice eggplants, salt them, and let sit for 30 minutes. Rinse and pat dry.

  2. Brush eggplant slices with olive oil, grill or bake until softened.

  3. Cook ground lamb with onion and garlic. Add tomatoes, cinnamon, oregano. Simmer 20 minutes.

  4. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).

  5. Drizzle with olive oil and balsamic glaze. Season with salt and pepper.

  6. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Serve with steamed vegetables or rice.
